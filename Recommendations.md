# Key Insights, Recommendations and Business Decisions

1. Electronics Sales Analysis

Insight:

- Electronics generated the highest net sales among all product categories, contributing around ₹4.75 crore.
- It shows that Electronics is an important category for the business.

Recommendation:

- Identify the best-selling products in the Electronics category.
- Maintain sufficient stock of products that have high demand.
- Monitor sales regularly to avoid stock shortages.

Business Decision:

- Give priority to high-demand Electronics products while planning inventory.
- Review product-wise sales before placing new stock orders.

How to measure the result: Track product-wise sales, stock availability and the number of out-of-stock items.

2. Discount Analysis

Insight:

- Orders with discounts had an average net order value of ₹2,740.
- Orders without discounts had a higher average net order value of ₹3,013.
- This shows that discounted orders had a lower average order value in this dataset.

Recommendation:

- Check whether discounts are actually helping the business increase sales.
- Test different discount offers instead of giving discounts to every customer.
- Compare sales and profit before and after offering discounts.

Business Decision:

- Continue, change or stop discount offers based on their actual performance.
- Focus on offers that increase sales without reducing profit too much.

How to measure the result: Compare sales, average order value, number of orders and profit during the discount campaign.

3. Customer Repeat Purchase Analysis

Insight:

- Around 55% of customers made more than one purchase.
- Repeat customers contributed around 76.4% of total sales.
- This shows that repeat customers are an important part of the business.

Recommendation:

- Introduce customer loyalty rewards.
- Provide special offers to existing customers.
- Send personalized offers based on customers' previous purchases.

Business Decision:

- Test a customer loyalty campaign to encourage more repeat purchases.
- Check whether the campaign increases customer purchases.

How to measure the result: Track repeat purchase rate, number of returning customers and sales from repeat customers.

4. City-wise Sales Analysis

Insight:

- Mumbai recorded the highest net sales at around ₹3.21 crore.
- Delhi was the second-highest city at around ₹3.11 crore.
- Bangalore recorded around ₹2.36 crore in net sales.

Recommendation:

- Study the products and customer preferences that contribute to higher sales in Mumbai.
- Understand why some cities generate more sales than others.
- Identify opportunities to improve sales in cities with lower sales.

Business Decision:

- Test successful sales strategies in other cities.
- Plan city-wise marketing activities based on customer demand.

How to measure the result: Compare city-wise sales, order numbers and average order value before and after the changes.

5. Busiest Shopping Hour Analysis

Insight:

- 7 AM was the busiest recorded shopping hour in the dataset.
- Other active shopping hours included 3 PM, 1 PM, 7 PM and 5 PM.

Recommendation:

- Study customer shopping patterns during these hours.
- Test special offers or marketing campaigns during busy shopping hours.
- Ensure that the website and payment process work smoothly during these periods.

Business Decision:

- Run a trial marketing campaign around busy shopping hours.
- Compare its results with other time periods before making it a regular activity.

How to measure the result: Compare hourly order numbers, sales and campaign performance.

6. Year-wise Sales Growth Analysis

Insight:

- Net sales increased from around ₹2.93 crore in 2020 to ₹3.39 crore in 2023.
- This represents an increase of approximately 15.4%.
- Average order value also increased during this period.

Recommendation:

- Identify which product categories and cities contributed most to sales growth.
- Study customer purchasing patterns during these years.
- Find the business activities that may have supported this growth.

Business Decision:

- Continue and expand strategies that show positive results.
- Focus on product categories and locations with good sales performance.

How to measure the result: Compare yearly sales, number of orders and average order value.

7. Payment Method Analysis

Insight:

- Credit cards were the most frequently used individual payment method.
- Around 40.2% of all orders used credit cards.
- Debit cards were the second most frequently used method.

Recommendation:

- Make sure popular payment methods work smoothly.
- Provide customers with multiple payment options.
- Monitor payment-related problems to improve the shopping experience.

Business Decision:

- Give priority to maintaining commonly used payment options.
- Review payment performance regularly.

How to measure the result: Track payment method usage, successful transactions and failed transactions.
