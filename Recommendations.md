Insights, Recommendations, and Decisions from My Retail Sales Analytics Project
1. Electronics Sales
- Insight: Electronics generated the highest net sales, around ₹4.75 crore.
- Recommendation: Monitor stock and identify popular electronics products.
- Decision to be taken: Prioritize stock for high-demand products.

2. Discount Analysis
- Insight: Discounted orders had a lower average net order value (₹2,740 compared to ₹3,013).
- Recommendation: Test whether discounts bring additional sales.
- Decision to be taken: Continue, change, or stop offers based on measured results.

3. Customer Repeat Purchases
- Insight: Around 55% of customers made multiple purchases.
- Recommendation: Encourage repeat purchases through loyalty rewards and personalized offers.
- Decision to be taken: Test a customer loyalty campaign.

4. City-wise Sales
- Insight: Mumbai had the highest net sales among all cities.
- Recommendation: Study the factors that drive sales in Mumbai.
- Decision to be taken: Test successful sales strategies in other cities.


5. Busiest Shopping Hour
- Insight: 7 AM was the busiest recorded shopping hour.
- Recommendation: Test marketing campaigns around busy shopping hours.
- Decision to be taken: Schedule a trial campaign and compare the results.

6. Year-wise Sales Growth
- Insight: Net sales increased from ₹2.93 crore in 2020 to ₹3.39 crore in 2023.
- Recommendation: Investigate which products, cities, or customers contributed to this growth.
- Decision to be taken: Invest further in strategies that show measurable results.


7. Payment Method Analysis
- Insight: Credit card was the most frequently used individual payment method.
- Recommendation: Ensure that popular payment options work smoothly.
- Decision to be taken: Prioritize the reliability of commonly used payment methods.
